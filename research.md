# Research

<div class="page-intro">My research centers on multimodal visual object tracking and robust cross-modal representation learning.</div>

<div class="research-detail-grid">
  <section class="detail-card">
    <span class="card-kicker">01</span>
    <h2>RGB-T Object Tracking</h2>
    <p>Visible and thermal infrared tracking under illumination variation, modality degradation, occlusion, background interference, and cross-modal discrepancy.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="detail-card">
    <span class="card-kicker">02</span>
    <h2>Multimodal Representation Learning</h2>
    <p>Adaptive fusion, modality reliability estimation, cross-modal interaction, and representation learning for heterogeneous visual signals.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="detail-card">
    <span class="card-kicker">03</span>
    <h2>RGB-Event Tracking</h2>
    <p>Complementary frame-event representation for robust tracking in fast motion and challenging illumination conditions.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="detail-card">
    <span class="card-kicker">04</span>
    <h2>Vision-Language Tracking</h2>
    <p>Language-guided visual tracking and multimodal semantic representation for stronger target understanding and generalization.</p>
  </section>
</div>
